# KLUNK – store listing (English, primary)

Source language for Google Play and the App Store. Swedish translation: `listing.sv.md`. Design basis: DESIGN.md §13–15.

## App name
KLUNK

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Drop, merge and grow glowing critters in a jar. Offline, no ads, no purchases.

## Long description (max 4000 characters)
Drop a little glowing critter into the jar. When two of the same kind touch, they merge into a bigger one. Keep merging to climb eleven levels, all the way up to the Klunk. Fill the jar past the line and the round is over. One more try takes less than a second.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Real physics: critters roll, bounce, stack and settle, so every drop matters.
- One finger: drag to aim, lift your finger to drop. No menus in the way.
- Chains and combos: one well-placed drop can set off merge after merge.
- Special pieces now and then, like a rainbow that merges with anything.

A COLLECTION BOOK
- Every level you create by merging is caught in your book.
- Now and then a critter comes out shimmering: a rare, purely cosmetic variant with its own sparkle and sound.
- Five theme sets to unlock by playing: the Glimmers, the Planets, the Frosties, the Candies and the Embers. Each has its own colours, sounds and particles. The rules never change.

48 FRIENDS
- Merging earns shells. Each shell holds a new friend who sits on the jar's rim and holds the next piece for you.
- 48 friends in six rarities. No duplicates: every shell gives someone new.
- Rarer friends have their own small abilities, from fireworks and echoes to a lantern that shows which pieces belong together.
- Friends grow through three levels as you play with them.

CALM AND FAIR
- Plays fully offline.
- No ads. No in-app purchases. No waiting timers, no energy, no daily streaks.
- No accounts and no data collection. Your progress is saved on your device only.
- Calm mode halves effects and turns off screen shake and zoom.
- No text needed to play, so it works for every age and language.

Suitable for ages 7 and up.
