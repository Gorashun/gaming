# Child-safety baseline review: KLUNK test version 8

Reviewer: child-safety-reviewer · Date: 2026-09-26 · Scope: everything that ships in test version 8 (DESIGN §1–18, UI §12–16, data files, Android manifest, store listing, e2e screenshots). This is the baseline that every new retention lane is judged against.

> This review is a design and policy-compliance assessment. It is not legal advice.

## Verdict: **PASS WITH CHANGES**

KLUNK is in good shape for a 7+ game. It has no real money, no ads, no network, no identifiers, and no timers, streaks or expiring offers. The shells are exhaustible and never give duplicates, the opening ceremony is fixed and skippable, the Shop badge follows the "free shell only" rule, and there is a flash guard with a unit test and a Calm mode. None of the current mechanics is a BLOCK.

Four things must be fixed before a public store release, and before new retention lanes are built on top of the shop and the round end:

1. The odds jars overstate rare odds by up to 8×.
2. The store listing makes claims that are no longer true, and the app has no privacy policy.
3. The near-miss pulse also fires for pairs that can never merge.
4. The loss screen has no on-screen way to stop, and there is no "good place to stop" moment anywhere.

The list of pre-approved patterns for new lanes is in §5.

---

## 1. Findings, in priority order

Severity: **P1** = fix before a public release or before new lanes build on the area. **P2** = fix within the next two test versions. **P3** = hygiene.

### P1-1 The odds jars overstate rare rarities (honesty of odds)
- **Where:** `oddsPearls()` in `data/avatars.ts` (the `Math.max(1, …)` floor), used by the mini jars in the shop (UI §14.3) and the pick3 shelf.
- **Problem:** Each rarity still in the pool gets at least 1 of 25 pearls. For the **common shell** that shows legendary as 4 % when it is 1.5 % (2.7× too high) and mythic as 4 % when it is 0.5 % (8× too high). The silver shell's mythic shows 4 % against 1.5 %. The jar is the game's only odds display, so the only odds a child ever sees are inflated, and inflated in the direction that sells the draw. The intent was to show that mythic exists, not to mislead, but the effect is the same. Apple 3.1.1 and Google's odds rule formally cover only paid boxes. Even so, "odds visible" is one of our own guardrails (TEAM rule 5), and the ICO Children's Code (std 13) and FTC 2022 both treat misleading presentation as a dark pattern.
- **Fix, both parts required:**
  1. **Jar:** a rarity whose true share is under 1/25 (4 %) gets **no full pearl**. Show it as a small hollow or dashed "speck" pearl at the top of the jar, meaning "exists, very rare" (this is the alternative in UI §14.10 q5). Full pearls then follow the true odds exactly (largest-remainder rounding).
  2. **Numbers:** add a small "i" button (≥64 px target) on the shop shelf that opens an odds sheet showing exact percentages per shell type, renormalised to what is left, for example "Legendary 1.5 %". A parent can read it and an older child learns from it. Text is allowed here under the UI §16.2 principle.
- **Test:** a unit test that `oddsPearls` never shows a share more than 1/25 above the true share for any full pearl, and an e2e screenshot of the odds sheet.

### P1-2 The store listing is out of date, and there is no privacy policy
- **Where:** `docs/store/listing.en.md` and `listing.sv.md`.
- **Claims that are no longer true (v1.3 economy and Start v2):**
  - "Merging earns shells." Merges now earn **pearls**. Shells come free at merge milestones or are **bought in an in-game Shop with pearls or star sand**.
  - "Friends grow through three levels as you play with them." They are now **upgraded by spending pearls and star sand** (DESIGN §16.3).
  - "No text needed to play." Start v2 has short labels. Say "Almost no reading needed" instead.
- **Missing clarity:** the app now has a screen called "Shop" and a "Free!" label, while the listing says "No in-app purchases". Add one plain sentence: "The shop uses only pearls and star sand earned by playing. Nothing can be bought with real money, and there are no ads." Parents and reviewers need this, and it matches the FTC 2022 concern about kids' apps that blur play and purchase.
- **Age line:** keep "PEGI 3 / IARC 3+ expected" **only as long as no play-by-appointment lane exists**. A daily gift or similar lane moves the expected rating to **PEGI 7** under the June 2026 criteria ("rewards for returning", sek.). Update the listing in the same release as that lane.
- **Privacy policy:** there is none in the repo. Google Play requires one for apps that target children (Families, sek.), and the App Store requires one for every app. Write a short policy: "KLUNK collects no personal data, makes no network connections, and stores progress only on this device." Host it at a URL, link it in both stores, and fill in Play's Data safety form as "No data collected, no data shared".
- **Copy:** "One more try takes less than a second." sells the restart loop, even if mildly. Replace it with a statement of fact such as "Rounds are short, a few minutes each."
- The legal reviewer must also see the new store text (TEAM rule 3).

### P1-3 Near-miss is only half genuine
- **Where:** `systems/nearmiss.ts` `findNearMiss`, called from `Game.checkNearMiss()`. Config in `data/juice.ts` (`minLevel` 8, gap 1–20 px).
- **Problem:** The code checks that **both objects are level ≥ 8** and that the gap is under 20 px. It does **not check that they are the same level**. A level 8 next to a level 9 therefore pulses as a "near miss" even though the two can never merge. That is a manufactured "almost", the thing DESIGN §5 rules out ("aldrig fejkad", never faked). Kikaren Kajsa lowers `minLevel` to 4, so the false pulses become much more frequent with her equipped.
- **Fix:** add `if (a.level !== b.level) continue;` before the gap test. Add a unit test for a level 8 and a level 9 at a 10 px gap that expects no match. Keep the rest: the pulse clears when the gap changes, and it is scale only with no brightness change.

### P1-4 The loss screen has no way to stop, and there is no "good place to stop" moment
- **Where:** `scenes/GameOver.ts`, UI §12.5 and §13.7.
- **Problem:** The whole screen is a restart button from t = 0, and the replay icon pulses forever (`repeat: -1`). The only way back to Start is Android's hardware back button, which many 7-year-olds do not know. iOS has no such button at all. Add the per-round tally, the "new!" flyers, the shell pop and the affordHint, and the game becomes a frictionless loop: play, harvest, instant replay. Every piece is harmless on its own. Together, with no exit on screen, they are the "sticky feature" pattern the ICO Children's Code describes (std 13, sek.). The ICO's 2023 guidance for game designers asks for break checkpoints (sek.).
- **Fix:**
  1. Add a **Home** button (`card` style, ≥64 px, label "Home"/"Hem") at the bottom of the loss screen, visually secondary to replay. Keep the full-screen tap = replay, but exclude the Home button's hit area from it. Restart stays under 0.5 s.
  2. Let the replay pulse run 3 cycles, then rest.
  3. Add a **gentle stop moment**. After about 20 minutes of play in a session, or 6 rounds in a row, the loss screen shows once per session a calm line with the buddy yawning or waving: "Nice run! Good place for a break." / "Bra runda! Ett bra ställe att ta paus." Rules: nothing is blocked or delayed, there is no timer and no reward for continuing or for stopping, and no guilt copy. Calm mode shows it too.
  4. Log `roundsThisSession` and `sessionMs` in the debug panel, so the playtest can see real session lengths.

### P2-1 "Shop" and "Free!" frame a no-money game as a store
- Label "Shop"/"Butik" with the sub-line "Free!"/"Gratis!" (START_STRINGS, screenshot `start-v2-badge.png`). There is no real money, so this is not a policy violation. But for a 7-year-old it trains the shopping frame, "Free!" is marketing language, and Apple 1.3 and Play reviewers scanning a kids app will reasonably ask where the purchases are.
- **Fix:** rename the card to **"Shells"/"Musslor"**, keeping the shell icon, and change the free sub-line to **"Gift!"/"Present!"** ("Gåva!"). Keep "Shop" out of player-facing text, and keep the shop shelf itself as it is.

### P2-2 The "you can afford it" hint in the round end uses the "shell earned" sound
- `GameOver.tallyRow` pops a small shell icon when pearls cross 300 and plays `AVATAR_SOUND.boxEarned`. The hint itself is acceptable: it happens once per crossing, with no pulse, no text and no repeat. But the sound tells a child that **a shell was earned** when none was. That is misleading, and it points the child at spending right at the restart moment.
- **Fix:** give the hint its own soft, neutral sound, or none. Show it as the shell with a **full progress ring** (the shop's "ready" language) rather than a shell that looks earned. Make sure it never fires twice for the same threshold (it can already fire only once per crossing, so keep it that way). Do **not** extend it to silver or gold.

### P2-3 The peeking shell bounces for as long as it waits
- UI §16.6 and §16.9: a bounce every 2 s for as long as a free shell is pending. The text of §16.9 says it "draws the eye once, then rests", but the data loops forever. That is a low-grade nag loop.
- **Fix:** 3 bounces each time Start is shown, then rest in the peeking pose. The badge and "Gift!" still carry the information.

### P2-4 The first shell is always rare but looks like a random draw
- `ECONOMY.firstShellRare`. A rigged first pull that looks like luck teaches "shells are good" through a staged outcome. It is a classic gacha onboarding technique, even if the intent here is only to show that abilities exist.
- **Fix:** make the first shell visibly **a gift, not a draw**: "choose your first buddy", pick 1 of 3 rares, reusing the pick3 UI. That keeps the onboarding goal, adds autonomy and removes the staged luck. At minimum, list "your first shell always contains a rare buddy" on the odds sheet (P1-1).

### P2-5 Pacing auto-drop: keep it, but measure it for children
- Auto-drop is hidden time pressure with no visible countdown (DESIGN §11–12). The floor is 3.5 s after 60 drops, and it runs only in Flow. The safeguards are good: it never runs while the finger is down (`!this.aiming`), never in danger, slow-mo or specials, never before the first own drop, and it is off in Calm mode. As difficulty design this is legitimate, not a retention mechanic.
- **Conditions:**
  1. Measure the auto-drop share for children in the playtest (target < 20 %, DESIGN §11). If it is higher, raise `rampEndMs` to 4 500 for the first 10 rounds of a player's life.
  2. Change the Calm-mode help text to "Less shake, flash and hurry" / "Mindre skak, ljus och stress", so parents can see that it also switches off auto-drop.
  3. Auto-drop must never be tied to meta state (economy, records, sessions).

### P2-6 Director Drought/Flow: fair, as long as it stays blind to the meta layer
- Drought avoids merge-ready levels with p 0.7 for 15–40 drops, and Flow prefers them with p 0.6. It is seeded, it controls only the queue, and it is not adaptive to spending, returning or skill. That is variable difficulty, like a Tetris randomiser, not manipulation.
- **Binding condition (for every future lane):** the director and the shiny, special and near-miss systems must **never** read economy, session, absence, "close to affording", "close to a record" or retention state. Add a unit test that the director output for a given seed is identical whatever the save contents.
- Tell parents on the odds/info sheet: "The game mixes calmer and livelier stretches; it is the same for everyone and never depends on the shop."

### P3-1 Android manifest and build hygiene
- The source manifest declares **no permissions**. The merged APK adds only `VIBRATE` (from `@capacitor/haptics`). `INTERNET` is correctly absent, and fonts are bundled locally. **PASS.**
- Remove the unused template `FileProvider` block. It is `exported=false`, so it is harmless, but it is dead surface.
- Remove the conditional `google-services` plugin block in `android/app/build.gradle`, so Firebase cannot slip in by someone adding a `google-services.json`.
- `allowBackup="true"`: Android Auto Backup copies the save file to the user's own Google account. It is not developer collection, so keep it (it protects progress). Add `dataExtractionRules`/`fullBackupContent` limited to the save file. Confirm the Data safety wording for backup when filling in the form (sek.; not verified at the source).
- **CI gate (release-engineer):** a step that runs `aapt dump permissions` on the built APK and fails on anything except `VIBRATE`. That turns the rule "a new permission = BLOCK" into an automatic check.

### P3-2 "Pearls" means three different things
- The currency, the rarity marks and the level-9 object are all called pearls (UI §14.10 q1). Adopt "stars" for rarity marks in all text, as DESIGN §16.5 already allows. This is for clarity, not a safety issue.

### P3-3 Round-end tally sound
- A rising-pitch tick for every round's harvest is acceptable (≤ 0.6 s, up to 12 ticks, soft). Keep the rule that it is never a coin clink and never lengthened for bigger hauls. It must not be extended with "double it" or bonus multipliers later (see BLOCK list).

## 3. Watch item: a random shell bought with earned currency

This is the one mechanic that sits closest to a line. A child accumulates pearls and then spends them on a random draw, and silver and gold are "pay more for better odds" tiers. Today it passes because:
- the currency cannot be bought, so it is not PRI under PEGI (sek.) and not a purchase under Apple or Google;
- the pool is exhaustible, there are no duplicates and every draw gives something new, so there is no "loss" outcome;
- there is no slot or gacha aesthetic.

The residual risks are Play's "simulated gambling" judgement, the EU Digital Fairness Act (proposal expected Q4 2026; restrictions on randomised rewards for minors are discussed, sek.), the CPC Network's key principles (Mar 2025, principle 7 on vulnerable consumers, sek.), and research showing that children read random rewards as "scams" even when free (CHI 2025, via avatar-box §4).

**Switch purchased shells to `SHOP.mode = 'pick3'`** (free shells stay random) if **any** of these happens:
1. A Play or App Store reviewer or the IARC questionnaire flags it.
2. The DFA text restricts randomised rewards for minors.
3. The playtest shows children buying shells back-to-back to chase a specific buddy, or calling it unfair.

The pick3 code is ready and persisted, so the switch is one flag. **No new random-draw lane may be added** (see BLOCK list) while this one is on watch.

## 4. Rules any new lane must keep (the invariants)

1. Nothing a player has ever earned is removed, reduced, decays or expires.
2. Nothing is available only for a limited time. Anything seasonal returns or stays obtainable.
3. Playing is always the best way to progress. Waiting or returning never beats one round of play.
4. No consecutive-day logic anywhere (count total days, never consecutive days).
5. No network, no identifiers, no new Android permission without a written review.
6. Nothing in any randomness system (director, shiny, specials, shells) reads economy, session, absence or retention state.
7. Every randomised reward shows honest odds (P1-1 rules) and is exhaustible, with no duplicates.
8. Every loop ≤ 1 Hz and registered in `JUICE.pulseHalfCycleMs`. Calm mode dampens it.
9. Copy is never guilt, urgency or FOMO: no "don't miss", "hurry", "last chance", "your buddy misses you", "X is waiting!". The only exclamation words allowed are for things already earned.
10. The listing and the IARC/PEGI answers are updated in the same release as the lane.

## 5. Pre-approved pattern list for new retention lanes

Use this list to design without a round-trip to me. Any lane still needs the normal gate (TEAM rule 3), but a spec that stays inside a PASS row with its numbers will be approved quickly. Rating impact is noted where it applies.

### PASS (approved as described)

| Pattern | Exact shape that is approved | Rating / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily gift that never expires and stacks** | One gift per local calendar day, **deterministic** (fixed pearls or sand, never random). Days you miss are banked, up to a cap of ≥ 7. Nothing already banked is ever removed. Value is the same on day 1 and day 100, or grows by **total** days played, never consecutive days. It must never be larger than about one round's pearls. No countdown to the next gift, no notification, and a Start badge only while a gift is actually waiting | PEGI 7 (play-by-appointment, positive, sek.). Update the listing |
| **Capped "while away" accumulator** | For example, the aquarium buddies gather pearls. It fills over **≥ 24 h** to a cap worth **≤ 1 round of play**, then just stops: no decay, no "full!" alert, no push. Collecting is one tap and no minigame. It must never be a random reward | PEGI 7 (sek.). The cap and the ≥ 24 h fill stop "check every few hours" habits |
| **Daily seeded challenge** | A date-seeded jar or queue that is the same for every player, offline. Optional and replayable without limit. Small one-time reward per day (sand or a cosmetic sticker). **Past days stay playable from an archive**, and missed days cost nothing. Personal best only, no leaderboards | PEGI 7 if a daily reward is attached (sek.) |
| **Free progression track** | A road or path driven by total merges or rounds. Never resets, has no season end and no premium lane. Every reward is reachable by play. It shows what is next, but there is no timer | No rating impact |
| **Achievements / stickers** | Skill, exploration and collection goals ("make a level 10", "try every set"). **Not** "play X days", "play at night", "open N shells" or "spend N pearls". No negative achievements | No rating impact |
| **Aquarium / home** | Arrange owned buddies and decorations. Buddies have **no needs**: no hunger, dirt or sadness, and no sad animation on return or exit. Decorations come from play, never from random draws | No rating impact |
| **Collection extensions** | New sets and book pages unlocked by play, with the same rules as §13 (unlock order may be random, but content is never missable) | – |
| **Personal records and replays** | Best score per set or buddy, "ghost" of your best run, polaroid gallery | – |
| **Wellbeing moments** | A "good place to stop" line (P1-4), an optional parent-set play-time reminder, pause/resume that never loses a run | Encouraged (ICO std 13 pro-wellbeing nudges, sek.) |

### PASS WITH CONDITIONS (approved only if **every** condition holds)

**A. Opt-in local reminders**
1. **Off by default.** Turning them on sits behind a simple parental gate (for example "hold for 3 s and solve 7 + 5"). Apple 1.3 expects parental gates for anything aimed at the adult.
2. **Local notifications only** (Capacitor LocalNotifications). No push service, no FCM or APNs token, no server.
3. Android 13+ needs `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`. That is a **new permission** and needs its own written review, and it may be requested only at the moment the parent turns the setting on, never at first launch.
4. At most **1 per day**, at a parent-chosen time between 08:00 and 19:00. Never on the day the game was already played.
5. Neutral copy only: "KLUNK is here if you want to play." / "KLUNK finns här om du vill spela." Never mention gifts waiting, expiring, buddies missing you, streaks or numbers.
6. They stop by themselves after 3 ignored reminders in a row, and can be switched off in one tap in Settings.
7. No reward is tied to opening the app from a notification.

**B. Missions / tasks**
1. **No expiry.** A mission stays until it is completed. Refreshes only *fill empty slots* (max 3 slots) and never remove an open mission.
2. The player can swap any mission for free, at any time, without limit.
3. Goals are about skill or variety ("make 3 chains of 3", "play a round with Frosties"). **Never** "play N rounds", "log in N days", "open shells", "spend pearls" or "upgrade a buddy".
4. Rewards are deterministic pearls or sand within the balance-analyst's economy sim (≤ about 1 round of pearls per mission). No random rewards, no exclusive items.
5. No "complete all 3 for a bonus chest" (that is a compounding loop).
6. No timers, no "daily" or "weekly" labels.

**C. Seasonal ambience**
1. Decoration only (snow in December, lanterns in autumn). No collectable, score or reward is tied to it.
2. If a seasonal cosmetic is ever collectable, it **stays obtainable all year** or returns every year, and the UI shows no dates or countdowns.

**D. "Welcome back" gift after absence**
1. Deterministic, the same size as or smaller than the daily gift, and **not** growing with the length of absence beyond the daily-gift bank.
2. Neutral copy ("Welcome back!"), no mention of what was "missed".
3. It must not stack on top of the daily gift into an absence-rewarding lump.

**E. New random rewards of any kind** (only once the §3 watch item is resolved)
1. Exhaustible, no duplicates, honest odds (P1-1 rules) with an exact-percentage sheet.
2. The same ceremony for every outcome, skippable. No step-up, no "almost", no "open all".
3. Never tied to returning (no random daily gift) and never bought with a currency that has any link to money.

**F. Session goals inside a run** ("reach level 8 this round")
1. Shown only at round start, optional, with no fail state or sound on failure.
2. The reward is small and deterministic. Never chained into "3 in a row".

### BLOCK (not approvable in any form)

| Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| **Streaks or streak loss**, consecutive-day multipliers, "day 7 bonus if you don't miss", streak freezes or repairs | Loss aversion on absence. PEGI 12 when progress is lost (sek.). 5Rights' streak case study (sek.). Breaks the store promise "no daily streaks" |
| **Expiring rewards or offers**, countdowns, "claim within 24 h", rotating stock that removes items, "last chance" | FOMO and time pressure. PEGI 12 for time-limited offers (sek.). ICO game guidance asks for risk assessment of time-limited offers (sek.). Breaks the store promise "no waiting timers" |
| **Limited-time exclusives**: seasonal-only buddies or sets, event passes, battle passes with an end date | Artificial scarcity aimed at children |
| **Energy, lives, tickets, cooldowns** or any gate on starting a round, including "wait or spend pearls to skip" | Gates play by time. Breaks the store promise "no energy" |
| **Loss while away**: decay, hungry or sad buddies, spoiling pearls, a dirty aquarium, a leaking accumulator | Penalty for absence, PEGI 12 (sek.). Guilt |
| **Social pressure**: leaderboards, friends, gifting requests, "share to get", "N players found this", comparisons | Needs network, identifiers and moderation. Social-comparison pressure (5Rights, sek.) |
| **Real money, ads, rewarded video, "watch to double"**, any purchasable currency | Guardrail 5. Makes shells PRI (PEGI 16, sek.). Apple 1.3 and Play Families |

| **Fake near-misses or "almost"** in any reveal, reel, wheel or spinner; stepwise rarity upgrades; candidates rolling past | Simulated gambling (Play Families, sek.). Our own rule "aldrig nästan" (never "almost") |
| **"Open all", auto-open, auto-restart or auto-play** of the next round | Removes the decision point. It is a sticky feature |
| **Spending or opening missions**, "buy X to unlock Y", bundles, "sale" or discount framing | Spending nudges aimed at children (FTC 2022, CPC principle 7, sek.) |
| **Duplicates, dust, infinite gacha, new random pools with no end** | Removes exhaustibility, the main safeguard of §3 |
| **Guilt or urgency copy and exit friction**: "Are you sure you want to leave?", sad goodbyes, hiding Home | Confirmshaming and roach-motel patterns (FTC 2022, sek.) |
| **Adaptive difficulty or odds tied to meta state** (easier after absence, luckier near a purchase or record) | Hidden manipulation. Breaks invariant 6 |
