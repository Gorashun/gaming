### 2.1 What was found (web search only)

### 2.2 Assessment

- The word **"klunk"** is onomatopoeia in English ("clunk") and an ordinary Swedish word ("a gulp"). That makes it relatively weak as a mark, but also free of an obvious identical owner in games.
- **KERPLUNK** is the one real close call. The spellings differ (K-L-U-N-K vs K-E-R-P-L-U-N-K) and so does the beginning. But both are onomatopoeia for something dropping, the goods overlap (games, now also online games), and Mattel enforces its marks. A tribunal would probably find the marks different overall, but that is not certain without a clearance opinion.
- Swedish meaning: "klunk" is also used for a swig of a drink, including alcohol. That is not an IP issue, just a note given the 7+ audience and "Drunk Klunk".

**Risk: MEDIUM (provisional)**, mainly because no registry could be searched and because of KERPLUNK.

### 2.3 What would lower it

1. A clearance search (EUTM, SE, UK, US, JP, WIPO Madrid) in classes 9, 28 and 41 for KLUNK and close variants (CLUNK, KLONK, KLUNKY, KERPLUNK).
2. If it comes back clear, file KLUNK (word, and later the logo) as an EUTM or at least a Swedish mark in 9/28/41 before the public launch.
3. In the store title, use a descriptive subtitle ("KLUNK: Merge Critters in a Jar"). Never use "Plunk", "Kerplunk" or any marble/stick imagery.

## 3. Similarity to Suika Game / Watermelon Game (Aladdin X)

### 3.1 The legal frame (short)

Game **rules and mechanics are not protected by copyright** (US 17 U.S.C. §102(b); in the EU and Sweden, ideas and functional rules are not protected, cf. CJEU C-406/10 *SAS Institute*; UK *Nova v Mazooma*). What can be protected is the **specific expression**: art, characters, layout and trade dress, sounds, text and names. US clone cases show where the line goes. *Tetris Holding v. Xio* (2012) found infringement where a clone copied the look (board, piece shapes and colours, visual details). *Spry Fox v. 6waves* (Triple Town vs Yeti Town, 2012) survived dismissal where a clone copied the overall selection and arrangement and settled. On top of copyright, the **store rules** matter. Apple 4.1 (Copycats) and Google Play's impersonation and repetitive-content policies are applied more loosely than courts, and Suika clones have been removed on request. Aladdin X publicly complained about clones in 2023. At that time "Suika Game" was reported as not registered as a trademark (AUTOMATON). That may have changed, and we should not use it anyway.

### 3.2 Side by side

| Element | Suika Game | KLUNK today | Protectable? | Our position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core loop: drop, same + same merge, lose on overflow | Yes | Yes | No (mechanic) | OK |
| Container | Wooden box on a warm beige, patterned background | Glowing glass jar on deep navy, set-specific backdrops | Expression | Different ✓ |
| Objects | 11 real fruits (cherry … watermelon) with faces | 11 abstract glowing critters, 5 themed sets. The same face per level across sets. The top tier is a gold crowned face | Expression | Different ✓, except for fruit names in two sets (below) |
| Tier count | 11 | 11 | No | Fine alone |
| Droppable tiers | 5 smallest | levels 0–4 (5 smallest) | No | Fine alone |
| **Score table** | 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, 45, 55, (66) | **1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, 45, 55, 66 + 500** (`DESIGN.md` §2) | Numbers are facts, but an **identical table is evidence of copying** | **Change it** |
| Top-tier pair | Two watermelons vanish | Two level-10 vanish (1000 pts, "double Klunk") | No | Fine, but together with the table the ruleset is 1:1 |
| Next preview | Top right, in a bubble | Top right, dashed box | Layout convention | OK. The dashed box is our own. |
| Score position | Top left | Top left | Convention | OK |
| Evolution display | "Circle of evolution" (a ring of fruits, bottom right) | A row of 11 silhouettes under the score, lit per round | Expression | Different ✓. Never make it a ring. |
| Danger line | Line near the top of the box | Dashed orange line, 1.5 s grace, slow-mo danger | No | OK |
| Extras | Paid skins, a later timer mode | Bomb, rainbow, 48 buddies with abilities, book, sets, auto-drop | – | Strong differentiation ✓ |

### 3.3 Assessment

The **look is clearly our own** (glow, jar, critters, buddies on the rim, navy palette). The **rules, however, are a complete copy of Suika's ruleset down to the score table**, and two sets contain fruit names. Neither is likely to be infringing in itself, but together they are exactly what a store reviewer, Aladdin X or a journalist would point at as "a Suika clone". **Risk: MEDIUM** (store rejection, a complaint to Apple or Google, reputation). It is cheap to fix.

### 3.4 Differentiation recommendations

**Rules and data (game-designer + balance-analyst, via the normal flow):**
1. **Own score curve.** For example, points = 2·n² + 2 per level created (2, 4, 10, 20, 34, 52, 74, 100, 130, 164, 202), or any curve that is not the triangular numbers. Rerun the balance sim. Highscores reset anyway on schema changes.
2. Optional, only if the design wants it: let the double-Klunk do something Suika's doesn't (for example burst into a pearl shower) rather than simply vanishing. Not required.

**Naming (UI data):**
3. Replace the fruit level names:

| Set | Level | Now EN / SV | Proposed EN / SV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planets | 3 | Peach / Persikan | Rosy Moon / Rosamånen |
| Candies | 2 | Lemon Drop / Citronen | Sherbet / Sorbeten |
| Candies | 4 | Orange / Apelsinen | Fizz Drop / Brustabletten |
| Candies | 5 | Blue Raspberry / Blåhallonet | Blue Fizz / Blåbubblan |
| Candies | 6 | Strawberry / Jordgubben | Pink Swirl / Rosavirveln |
| Candies | 7 | Sour Apple / Suräpplet | Sour Pop / Surklubban |
| Candies | 8 | Blueberry / Blåbäret | Gumdrop / Gelébiten |

4. Never draw a green-striped round object as a top tier, and never use a red/green "watermelon" palette pairing.

**Store text, ASO and screenshots:**
5. Never use "Suika", "watermelon", "fruit", "fruit merge", "like Suika" or "スイカ" in the title, subtitle, keywords, description, ads, social posts or file names. (The current listing complies ✓.)
6. **Screenshots:** screenshot 1 should show the buddy on the rim plus the Book or Shells, not a bare jar. At most one gameplay-only shot, and it should use the Glimmers or Planets set rather than Candies. Show the bomb and rainbow in action and the set variety. Use captions in our own voice ("48 buddies to collect", "Five glowing worlds").
7. Icon: a KLUNK critter with its buddy, not a round fruit-like ball in a box.

## 9. What a lawyer should confirm before a public store release

1. **Name clearance:** a full search for KLUNK (and the chosen fallback) in EUTM, Swedish PRV, UK, US, Japan and WIPO Madrid, classes 9/28/41. Ask specifically about **KERPLUNK (Mattel)**, and whether to file KLUNK before launch.
2. **Suika:** confirm that after the §3.4 changes (own score curve, no fruit names, store and screenshot rules) the game is sufficiently distinct, including for **Japan** if it will be released there, and for Apple guideline 4.1.
3. **Renames:** confirm the MEDIUM renames (Siri → Vera, Freddie → Figgy, Pirate Pete → Captain Kip, Frosties → Icelings/Isbitarna) and the Keroppi and Mickey look fixes are enough.
4. **Open-source compliance:** confirm that the notices approach (in-app Licenses screen + `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`, including the Apache-2.0 text and Cordova's NOTICE) meets MIT/ISC/BSD/Apache-2.0/OFL.
5. **Privacy:** the privacy policy text, Google Play Data safety and the Apple privacy label for a no-data, offline app aimed at 7+. Confirm the GDPR (Art. 8 / IMY) and COPPA position given no data processing, and whether `allowBackup` matters.
6. **Ratings and loot-box rules:** whether shells (random buddies bought only with earned, never-purchasable pearls or star sand) are outside "paid random items" under PEGI's June 2026 criteria (a PEGI 16 trigger) and under Belgian/Dutch loot-box law. How to answer the IARC questionnaire (random items: yes; paid: no). PEGI 7 for daily lanes.
7. **Store programmes:** Google Play Families policy (a target audience that includes under-13s) and Apple Kids-category rules, if chosen.
8. **Ownership of AI-assisted content:** code, art recipes, names and store text were largely produced with AI assistance. Under US Copyright Office guidance and Swedish law (which requires human creative choices), protection may be thin, which matters for enforcing against clones of KLUNK itself. Confirm how to document human authorship, and that all contributors assign rights to the publishing entity.
9. **Publisher identity:** the developer name on the stores (a private person vs a company; the app ID is `se.ferrer.klunk`), EU DSA "trader" status on the App Store, and the contact address shown publicly.
