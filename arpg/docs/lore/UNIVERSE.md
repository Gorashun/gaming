## 0. Tone guide (read this first)

**The one-sentence rule:** a 7-year-old understands it, and an adult smiles.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short lines. One idea per line. 12 words is a lot. | Paragraphs in UI. Lore dumps before combat. |
| Spooky = empty, quiet, grey, forgotten. | Blood, gore, wounds, body horror, "dead" bodies. |
| Enemies **go out**, get **snuffed**, get **rekindled**, **pop into sparks**, **run home**. | "Kill", "slay", "murder", "die" in player-facing text. (Code may say `kill`.) |
| Monsters are lost, not evil. Bosses have a sad, kind reason. | Villains who are cruel for fun. |
| Humour lives in details: a label, a sock, a grammar correction. | Jokes that punch down, crude words, jokes about bodies. |
| The dark is rest gone wrong. Rest itself is good. | Guilt about stopping. "Come back or lose it!" |
| Invite: "You could…", "If you like…", "Whenever you're ready." | Pressure: "Hurry!", "Don't miss out!", "Last chance!", timers, "limited". |
| Plain English. Invented words from our own vocabulary (wick, glow, hush, dim, kindle, lantern). | Other games' capitalised nouns (see BLOCKLIST). "Ember" as a resource name. "Rift" as a mode. |

**Words we use:**
- **Wickbearer** — the player. Someone who still carries a flame.
- **Wickwright** — a master of wicks and lanterns. The old guild. What the player becomes at the end.
- **The Hush** — the grey quiet that spreads where light is gone.
- **The Snuffed** — creatures whose inner light went out. They forgot who they were.
- **Rekindle** — what happens when you defeat one. It pops into sparks and runs home, remembering.
- **Sparks** — pieces of the broken World-Lantern flame. Loot "remembers" because it is made of sparks.
- **Kindle** — how much spark an item still holds for crafting.
- **Brightness** — how brightly you shine after level 60. Your light circle grows.
- **Lightwells** — deep wells where old light pooled. The Hush filled them. You dive in.

**Voice by audience:**
- *Kids hear:* "The dark makes people sad and forgetful. Light helps them remember."
- *Adults hear:* a story about loneliness, burnout, grief and the kindness of sharing the load.

## 1. Cosmology

### The world: Lumenholm
Lumenholm is a round, small, cosy world. Bogs, woods, mountains, a castle. Five places you can walk between.

It has no sun. For as long as anyone remembers, the sky held **the World-Lantern**: a great lantern hanging from nothing, warm as a kitchen window.

### What light does in Lumenholm
Light is not just for seeing. **Light remembers.**
- Where light falls, things keep their **colour**.
- Where light falls, things keep their **sound**.
- Where light falls, people keep their **memories** — their names, their songs, their friends.

Every living thing carries a tiny light inside, called its **wick**. Your wick is lit by the World-Lantern, the way a candle is lit by another candle.

### The Hush
Before the Lantern, there was only **the Hush**. Not evil. Not angry. Just *nothing much*. Like a room after everyone has gone to bed.

The Hush is patient. It does not chase. It **seeps**. It fills any place where light is missing, the way water fills a hole.

Where the Hush sits too long:
1. Colours fade to grey.
2. Sounds go soft, then stop.
3. Memories drift away. First small ones (where you left your hat). Then big ones (your name).

A creature whose wick goes out in the Hush becomes **Snuffed**. It does not know itself. It only knows that light *hurts a bit* — the way morning hurts when you're sleepy — so it tries to put light out.

### The truth about the Hush (Act 5 reveal)
The Hush is not a monster. **It is rest without anyone to wake up.** In the old days the Lantern dimmed each evening, and the Hush came in gently as *night*: sleep, dreams, quiet. It was good. The trouble began when the Lantern went out completely — night with no morning.

The ending does not destroy the Hush. It **gives the night back its morning.**

## 2. History (timeline)

| Era | What happened | Where players meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Before** | Only the Hush. | Act 5 skybox; journal page "Before". |
| **The Wandering Spark** | A single spark drifted in from nowhere. The Hush said nothing, so the spark kept glowing. It is the first thing that ever *remembered*. | Storyteller tales; the Moth Choir sings it. |
| **The First Wickwrights** | Small folk found the spark and built a lantern around it so it wouldn't blow away. They hung it high so everyone could share it. The **Wickwright Guild** was born. | Lastlight Landing; journal pages. |
| **The Age of Lanterns** (~1,000 years) | Towns grew under the light. Each town dug a **Lightwell** to catch spare light, like rain barrels. Bells rang evening and morning. | Hub towns; Lightwells. |
| **The Long Watch** | Fewer people joined the Guild. "Someone else will do it." In the end one keeper, **Pell**, tended the Lantern alone. For a very, very long time. | Act 5; the Tenth Lantern secret. |
| **The Long Breath** (the Snuffing) | One night, Pell — now grey, tired and hollow — blew the Lantern out. The flame burst into thousands of **sparks** that rained over Lumenholm. People called the stranger who did it **the Grey Guest**. | Opening cinematic; Act 1 intro. |
| **The Dimming** (now) | The Hush seeps out of the sky and up from the Lightwells. Towns huddle around their last lamps. A few people still glow. You are one of them. | The game. |
| **The Rekindling** (after Act 5) | The Lantern is relit — smaller, shared, and with a proper night again. The Hush retreats to the **Deepdark**. | Endgame. |

## 3. The World-Lantern

- A lantern the size of a hill. Glass panes, a brass cap, a chain that goes up into nowhere.
- Its **Lantern Room** is reached by a spiral stair from the bottom of the **Well of Hush** — the world is upside down there. You climb *down* to reach the sky.
- It needs a keeper to trim its wick each night. That is the whole job. It is a small job. It is also forever, if you do it alone.
- **Theme:** the light was never meant for one person to carry. The ending relights it with many small lights — yours, the towns', even the Guest's.

## 4. Factions and peoples

### The Lamplighters (the towns)
Ordinary folk who stayed. Every town keeps a few lamps burning with scraps of wick and stubbornness. They run the smithies, inns and stashes. They are funny, tired and brave. **They are why you keep going.**

### The Wickwright Guild (what's left)
Once the keepers of the Lantern. Now: one old master (Ada Brightly, Act 5), a lot of empty chairs, and a very long rulebook nobody reads. The Guild's motto: *"A light shared is not a light lost."*

### The Snuffed (enemies)
Creatures whose wicks went out. They are not evil — they forgot. Each act has its own family.

| Act | Family | Members (suggested monster ids) | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Wickmire | **Rattlebones** (+ bog critters) | `rattler`, `shieldbones`, `bog_archer`, `candle_mage`, `bonepile`, `warden`; bog extras `mud_frog`, `mist_snail`, `lily_ghost` | Old graveyard skeletons who forgot they were supposed to be sleeping. They rattle politely. |
| 2 Whisperwood | **Twiglings** | `twigling`, `owlcrow`, `moss_hound`, `puffcap`, `thorn_sprite`, `stump_grumble` | Stick-folk and forest critters who whisper gossip about you. Most of it is wrong. |
| 3 Echo Mines | **Diglings** | `digling`, `crystal_spider`, `cart_mimic`, `echo_bat`, `salt_golem`, `lamp_ghost` | Miners and mine-things who forgot why they dig. They keep digging anyway. |
| 4 Rimehall | **The Frozen Court** | `porcelain_doll`, `tin_soldier`, `mirror_butler`, `frost_jester`, `ice_courtier`, `snow_hound` | A royal party frozen mid-toast. Very polite. Very cold. |
| 5 Well of Hush | **Hushlings** | `hushling`, `sound_eater`, `grey_moth`, `faded_knight`, `hollow_choir`, `nobody_hat` | Shapes with no faces and no sound. Everything goes quiet near them. |

**Defeat rule (all Snuffed):** squash → spark burst → one little ember flies to you → the creature, now tiny and glowing, squeaks "thank you!" and runs home.

### Magpie Imps (neutral)
Small, greedy, giggling imps in magpie cloaks. They collect **shiny sparks** and hide them in the **Magpie's Hoard**, a pocket-world nest of glittering junk. They never fight. They run. Catch one and it drops gold — and sometimes forgets to close the door to the Hoard behind it.
*Secret:* one reformed Magpie Imp, **Nib**, runs the trading post at Lastlight Landing. "I don't steal. I *borrow permanently*."

### The Moth Choir (neutral, helpful)
Thousands of moths who love every light. They sing the old stories in tiny voices. They are the game's storytellers in Act 5 and the source of secret hints everywhere. Where moths gather, something is hidden.

### The Grey Guest (antagonist → friend)
A tall, thin figure in a grey coat and a hat with no brim. No face you can remember. Every town has a story about him knocking at a door, asking to come in "just for a moment, just to sit down". See §6.5.

## 5. The player: the Wickbearer
When the Lantern went out, a few people's wicks *kept burning*. Nobody knows why. (Secret: see §9, the Answering Mailbox.) You are one of them.

Your light makes a warm circle around you — the game's signature visual. Snuffed creatures step into it and *remember* for a moment. As you grow in **Brightness**, your circle grows.

The Guild gives you a small brass lantern and one rule: **"Carry the light. Share it when you can. Rest when you need to."**

## 6. The five acts

Each act: a hub town, three zones, a boss arena. Monster ids for new acts are proposals for the systems/data owners.

### 6.1 Act I — Wickmire *(bog / graveyard)*
**Theme:** the first dark. A stilt-village where the lamps float away like lost balloons.
**Boss:** The Sunken Bellringer · **Station:** Smith

| Zone id | Name | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| `a1_town` | **Lamplight Hollow** | A village on stilts. The last lamps are tied down with string so they can't float off. |
| `a1_z1` | **The Drowned Graves** | The old graveyard slid into the bog. The Rattlebones woke up confused and wet. First kill, first loot. |
| `a1_z2` | **Bellcrypt Halls** | Crypts under the bell tower. A Magpie Imp is always here, stealing sparks. |
| `a1_z3` | **The Sunken Nave** | The half-sunk chapel. You hear a bell under the water. Everyone near it yawns. |
| `a1_boss` | **Belfry of the Drowned** | The top of the sunken tower, poking out of the lake. The Bellringer waits. |

**Boss backstory — The Sunken Bellringer.** Old **Tolly Clapperton** rang the evening bell every night for sixty years: *"Come home, come home, the dark is coming."* The night the Lantern went out he rang and rang so everyone would get home safe. Nobody could see the way. He kept ringing. The tower sank. Tolly turned to stone from sheer worry, still ringing. Now the Hush lives in his bell, and every *DONG* makes people fall asleep where they stand.
**The kind reason:** he was trying to call everyone home.
**Rekindling:** his stone cracks like an egg. Tolly, small and blinking, rings one bright note — the *morning bell*. The floating lamps of Wickmire drift back down. He moves to Lamplight Hollow and rings the town bell whenever you return. (Ambient: bell chime on hub arrival after Act 1.)

### 6.2 Act II — Whisperwood *(grey birch forest, giant mushrooms, owl towers)*
**Theme:** secrets. Trees gossip about you. Shadows are missing.
**Boss:** Mother Bark · **Station:** Alchemist

| Zone id | Name | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| `a2_town` | **Owlstead** | A village of rope bridges and treehouses, lit by jars of glowworms. |
| `a2_z1` | **The Gossiping Birches** | The trees whisper about you. "Look at their boots." "I heard they're scared of moths." |
| `a2_z2` | **Puffcap Hollow** | Giant glowing mushrooms. Puff! Sparkly spores. Things hide under caps. The Tea Party is behind the waterfall here. |
| `a2_z3` | **The Owl Towers** | Old stone towers full of Owlcrows who turn their heads all the way round. Nobody here has a shadow. |
| `a2_boss` | **Mother Bark's Pantry** | A clearing full of shelves. On the shelves: thousands of jars. In the jars: shadows. |

**Boss backstory — Mother Bark.** The oldest birch in the forest. She tucked the wood in every night and hummed it to sleep. A shadow only exists where there is light — so when the Lantern went out, every shadow in the wood began to fade away. Mother Bark could not bear it. She caught them, one by one, and kept them safe in jars. Now she thinks *you* are here to steal them.
**The kind reason:** she was saving the shadows so they wouldn't vanish.
**Rekindling:** your lantern casts *her* shadow for the first time in a year. She laughs — a creaky, happy laugh — and opens every jar. Shadows zoom home to their owners. She gives you a sleepy acorn: "Plant it someday. Somebody in there needs a long nap." (Hook for **Rootweaver**.)

### 6.3 Act III — Echo Mines *(salt and crystal mines, rails, echoing halls)*
**Theme:** forgetting. Miners who forgot why they dig.
**Boss:** The Great Echo · **Station:** Jeweler

| Zone id | Name | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| `a3_town` | **Clinkerton** | A mining camp built on a giant lift. It goes up. It goes down. Mostly it creaks. |
| `a3_z1` | **Saltwhistle Shafts** | White salt tunnels. The wind whistles tunes nobody remembers. |
| `a3_z2` | **The Glittering Gallery** | Crystal caves. Crystal Spiders clink when they walk. If it goes quiet, look up. |
| `a3_z3` | **Rattletrack Rails** | Mine-cart tracks in the dark. One of the carts has teeth. |
| `a3_boss` | **The Mouth of the Mountain** | A cave so big it has weather. It copies everything you do — backwards. |

**Boss backstory — The Great Echo.** The mountain itself had a voice. For a thousand years it sang the miners' work-song back to them: *"Dig deep, dig bright, bring the shine to light."* When the lamps went out, the miners forgot the song. The Echo had nothing to repeat. It got so lonely that it started copying *everything* — footsteps, sneezes, swings of a hammer — and it copies them wrong, backwards, and much too loud.
**The kind reason:** it just wanted someone to talk to.
**Rekindling:** the miners (freed from the Snuffed) remember the first line of the song. The Echo sings it back, right way round. The whole mountain hums. Secret: stand completely still for 30 seconds during the fight and the Echo has nothing to copy. It gets confused, then curious, then drops the **Echo of Stillness**.

### 6.4 Act IV — Rimehall *(frozen castle, ballrooms, mirrors)*
**Theme:** loneliness. A royal party frozen in the middle of a toast.
**Boss:** The King Who Forgot His Name · **Station:** Runecarver

| Zone id | Name | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| `a4_town` | **Kettlekeep** | The castle kitchens — the only warm rooms left. Everyone who stayed lives here, next to the big kettle. |
| `a4_z1` | **The Frosted Gardens** | Hedges shaped like animals, frozen solid. Some of them are not hedges. |
| `a4_z2` | **The Looking-Glass Gallery** | Halls of mirrors. Your reflection is a little bit late. Mirror Butlers serve tea. The tea is a trap. They are very sorry. |
| `a4_z3` | **The Everlasting Toast** | The great banquet hall. Hundreds of frozen guests, cups raised, mid-cheer. |
| `a4_boss` | **The Throne of Frost** | An icy throne room. A king who asks: "Who are you?" And then: "Who am *I*?" |

**Boss backstory — The King Who Forgot His Name.** **King Aldric Warmhearth** was famous for the warmest hall in Lumenholm. Anyone could come to dinner. On the night of the Long Breath he raised his cup: *"To the Lantern, and to all of you —"* and the light went out mid-sentence. The cold rushed in. Everyone froze. The king forgot the end of his toast, then his guests, then his own name. The less he remembers, the colder — and stronger — he gets.
**The kind reason:** he is trying to remember who he loves.
**Rekindling:** in the zones before the boss you collect four **name-letters** (A-L-D-R… the fight gives the rest). In the last phase you say his name. He stops. His crown thaws. He finishes his toast: *"— and to all of you, who stayed."* His daughter, **Princess Pip** (Kettlekeep's pet keeper), runs in. He remembers her first.

### 6.5 Act V — The Well of Hush *(upside-down starry void, floating leftovers of every act)*
**Theme:** the truth. The world behind the Lantern. Everything is grey except you, your loot and danger.
**Boss:** The Grey Guest · **Station:** Cauldron

| Zone id | Name | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| `a5_town` | **Lastlight Landing** | A lighthouse on the rim of the Well. The Guild's last hall. The only warm light for miles. |
| `a5_z1` | **The Upside-Down Stars** | You walk on the sky. The stars are under your feet. Some of them are lanterns that fell. |
| `a5_z2` | **The Drift of Leftovers** | Floating islands made of bits of every act: a bog stair, a birch, a mine-cart, a ballroom door. Things the Hush took. |
| `a5_z3` | **The Quiet Stair** | A spiral stair going down to the sky. No sound at all. Your footsteps come back as tiny lights. |
| `a5_boss` | **The Lantern Room** | Inside the dark World-Lantern. A small grey figure sits on the edge of the cold wick. |

**Boss backstory — The Grey Guest.** His name was **Pell**. He was the last Wickwright who stayed. For hundreds of years he climbed the stair each night and trimmed the wick. Alone. People looked up and said "how pretty" and never asked who kept it lit. He grew tired. Then more than tired — hollow. He began to fear the light: as long as it burned, he could never stop. One night he thought: *"If it goes out, maybe someone else will finally have to carry a light."* He blew.
Then he saw what the dark did, and he was too ashamed to go back up. He wandered the world as the Grey Guest, knocking on doors, asking to sit down.
**The kind reason:** he was exhausted, and nobody noticed. He wanted to be seen — and to rest.
**The fight:** Pell doesn't want to hurt you. The Hush that filled him does. Phases: (1) he fights with the Lantern's cold chain; (2) he summons grey copies of every boss you rekindled — each one steps aside when it sees you ("Oh! It's you. Thank you."); (3) the Lantern Room goes silent and only your circle of light remains.
**Rekindling (not destroyed):** you do not strike the last blow. The final prompt is **"Share your light."** You light *his* small, old lantern from yours. Colour floods back into his coat. He sits down. He cries a little. He says: *"You carried it. I thought no one would."* The World-Lantern is relit — not by one keeper, but by every light the player rekindled on the way, shining up from below.
**Epilogue:** night returns — real night, with a morning at the end of it. Pell retires to Lastlight Landing, where he trims the lighthouse wick *once a week*, "because it's nice, not because I must." The player is named **Wickwright**.

## 7. Endgame — The Deepdark
After the Rekindling, the Hush has not vanished. It sank down, *under* everything, into the **Deepdark** — the underside of every act, where the forgotten things still sleep.

- **Deepdark runs:** the five acts again, but the Hush has seeped back in. Greyer. Quieter. Stronger Snuffed. Keyed runs use **Lantern Maps** with modifiers ("Foggy", "Echoing", "Upside-down").
- **Lightwells:** the town wells that drained into the Deepdark. Dive in, gather as much light as you can in a few minutes, climb out. A calm "Good place to rest" waits at the top. If you stop halfway, the light you gathered still counts.
- **Lightwell Trials:** the Guild's old tests. Deeper wells, for the brave. Scores are kept on the Guild's chalkboard (local).
- **Brightness (levels 61–200):** each level makes your light a little brighter and your circle a little bigger. The starmap shows the old Guild constellations: The Kettle, The Sleeping Hare, The Crooked Stair, The Two Lanterns, The Moth… (starmap designers own the full list).
- **Weekly boss (proposal):** **The Long Shadow** (`long_shadow`) — every shadow Mother Bark couldn't save, gathered into one. Its first defeat each week gives a bonus; missed weeks bank up to 3.
- **Level 200 beat:** the last letter in the Answering Mailbox (§9).

**Endgame tone:** the Deepdark isn't a war. It's tidying up after a very long night. Pell sometimes comes along, in a small window, to say something kind.

## 8. The classes

Every class can use every weapon (GDD §19). The personalities show up in hero barks, hub greetings and journal marginalia.

### Lanternbearer *(Knight model · tank / holy fire · Glow)*
- **Origin:** Lamplight Hollow. The last trainee of the Lamplighters, taught by Warden Hollis Reed. She got the Guild's biggest lantern because nobody else could lift it. It swings on a chain.
- **Personality:** the calm big sister. Always says "we've got this". Secretly scared of the dark — that's *why* she carries the biggest light.
- **Suggested default name:** Maren.
- **Catchphrases:** "We've got this." · "Stay in the light. Stay close." · "Nothing to be scared of. …Mostly." · "Brighter together." · (low health) "Okay. Okay. Still glowing."

### Bellstriker *(Barbarian model · berserker, AoE · Fury)*
- **Origin:** Clinkerton. Raised by the bell-founders of the mines; his aunt is Dorra Anvilsong. His hammer has a bell for a head. Every hit rings. Snuffed creatures wobble when they hear it.
- **Personality:** LOUD. Huggy. Cries openly at sad songs. Terrified of spiders, which is awkward in Act 3.
- **Suggested default name:** Brom.
- **Catchphrases:** "DING!" · "Group hug after this!" · "Is that a spider? That's a spider. THAT'S A SPIDER." · "Everybody wake UP!" · "Sorry! Too loud? Sorry!"

### Stargazer *(Mage model · ranged elements · Mana)*
- **Origin:** Owlstead's observatory, apprentice to Old Hoot. When the Lantern went out, the stars were the only light left. She learned to pull constellations down and throw them.
- **Personality:** nervous bookworm, glasses too big. Corrects bosses' grammar mid-fight.
- **Suggested default name:** Isolde.
- **Catchphrases:** "Technically, that's a nebula." · "It's 'whom', actually." · "I read about this! …The book didn't end well." · "Star light, star bright — duck!" · "Please hold still, I'm calculating."

### Stitcher *(Rogue model, warm patchwork · summoner · Thread)*
- **Origin:** Wickmire. Granddaughter of Grandmother Purl, the storyteller. She sews cloth dolls from lost socks and buttons. They wake up and fight for her. (She's the kind kind of doll-maker.)
- **Personality:** talks to her dolls non-stop. They answer. Slightly creepy, totally delighted.
- **Suggested default name:** Tibby.
- **Catchphrases:** "Say hello, Mister Buttons." · "Snip, snip, stitch!" · "The dolls say you're very brave. One of them says you're very short." · "Nobody gets left unmended." · "Oh, she likes you!"

### Roofrunner *(Rogue_Hooded model · agile, traps, crossbow · Energy + combo)*
- **Origin:** the rooftops of Rimehall. A chimney kid who ran messages across the castle roofs. Best friend of Princess Pip. Escaped the freeze by being on the roof, as usual.
- **Personality:** cocky. Brags about things that never happened. Pinches small things from everyone, including you. Always gives them back. Mostly.
- **Suggested default name:** Kit.
- **Catchphrases:** "Saw that coming. Totally." · "I once jumped over a whole castle. Twice." · "Is this yours? It's mine now. Kidding! …Kidding." · "Up and over!" · "Quick, quiet, quality."

### Rootweaver *(expansion · shapeshifter druid · Sap)*
- **Origin:** the acorn Mother Bark gives you. Plant it in the Deepdark and out comes someone as old as the forest who has been napping for a few hundred years.
- **Personality:** sleepy, forgetful, gentle. Sometimes falls asleep mid-sentence. Shapeshifts into a moss-bear, a beetle swarm, a stag.
- **Suggested default name:** Old Oakley.
- **Catchphrases:** "Mm? Oh. Yes. Fighting." · "Slow roots, strong roots." · "I remember when this was a seed. So was I." · "Just resting my eyes… for a century." · "The beetles know the way."
- **Legal note:** "Rootweaver" is the GDD working name. "Woven / the Weaver" are blocked (Last Epoch); keep the compound only, never "the Weaver", and include it in the name review.

## 9. Mysteries and secrets
Rewarding curiosity, never schedules. All timing uses the **in-game** moon and weather, never the device clock (welfare).

1. **The Tenth Lantern.** Every hub has nine lampposts and one empty bracket. On in-game full-moon nights, a tenth lantern hangs there, dark. Light it and a small door opens: a tiny room where the Grey Guest (before Act 5) or Pell (after) sits by a fire. He talks about what you've done. He knows more than he should. Drops **Moonthread**.
2. **The Answering Mailbox.** An old mailbox in every act. The letters are addressed to *you* and warn about things that haven't happened yet ("Mind the third mirror."). They're signed with a drawn lantern. The last letter, at Brightness 200, reveals the sender: **you**, from another turn of the story. (Why your wick kept burning: you lit it yourself, from the other side.)
3. **The Tea Party Behind the Waterfall** (Puffcap Hollow). Some Snuffed are having tea. They don't attack. Sit in the empty chair and don't touch the controls for a minute: you get a cosmetic hat and a bestiary entry: *"Snuffed, but lovely."*
4. **The Frog That Never Jumped** (Wickmire). On a lily pad, only in in-game rain. It hops away if you run. Walk. Needed for *The Frog King's Croak*.
5. **The Owlcrow's Riddles** (Whisperwood). Owlcrows sometimes ask riddles. After 50 right answers in a row, one asks the question with no answer. The right reply is "I don't know." Gives **An Answer Nobody Knew**.
6. **Echo of Stillness** (Mouth of the Mountain). Stand still for 30 seconds while the Great Echo copies you.
7. **The Candy Crypt.** A rare cracked wall in Bellcrypt Halls opens into a crypt made of sweets. The Rattlebones there are made of sugar and very embarrassed.
8. **The Magpie's Hoard.** Catch a Magpie Imp and it may leave its door open. A nest of glittering junk and real treasure. Mind the imps' grandmother. She's asleep. Mostly.
9. **The Mystery Egg.** Sometimes you find a warm egg. Carry it. It hatches after enough adventures. What hatches depends on where you've been.
10. **The Hub Cat.** Every town has the same grey cat. It is always asleep, and only purrs when the in-game moon is full. Pet it gently. (It is not the Grey Guest. Probably.)
11. **Pell's Name.** The Grey Guest's real name is hidden in pieces across 5 journal pages. Finding all 5 before Act 5 adds one extra line to the ending: the player calls him by name before he says it.
12. **The Empty Chairs.** Lastlight Landing's Guild hall has 99 chairs. After the ending, one chair has your name carved on it.

## 10. Towns and their people (overview)

## 11. Items and loot in the fiction
- **Loot is sparks.** When the World-Lantern shattered, its flame fell as sparks. Sparks cling to things and make them *remember*. That's why items have flavour and powers.
- **Rarity = how much the item remembers.** Common: a little. Legendary: a whole story. Mythic: a memory so bright it's almost a person. **Unique**: remembers one specific person. **Named**: crafted from memories that should be impossible to hold.
- **Kindle** = spark left for crafting. Spend it at the stations.
- **Upgrading (+0 to +15)** at the Smith: "polishing the memory". It never fails.
- **Salvage**: gently letting the spark go back into materials.
- **Materials** in-fiction: Soot Dust (cold soot), Wickthread (unburnt wick), Dusk Essence (bottled evening), Ember Heart (a warm core — prose name only, not a currency), Bone Meal, Bell Shard.

## 12. Writing checklist (per line)
1. Could a 7-year-old read it aloud? (Short words, ≤ 12 words for barks and tips.)
2. Does it avoid kill/die/blood/dead in player-facing text?
3. Does it avoid pressure (hurry, last chance, don't miss, limited, streak, expires)?
4. Is every proper noun ours and BLOCKLIST-clean?
5. Is there one small smile in it somewhere?
