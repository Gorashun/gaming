# BLOCKLIST: names and expressions we must not use

> Owner: legal-counsel. Date: 2026-09-26. Applies to all text, item and skill names, UI labels, store copy, file and asset names shown to players, and marketing.
> Principle: **mechanics are free, expression is not.** Difficulty tiers, rarities, rifts, cubes and paragon-style levels are unprotected *ideas*. Their **names, text, icons, UI layouts, sounds and distinctive combinations** are protected expression and/or trademarks. Where a term is not verified as a registered mark, we still block it: the goal is to avoid *passing off* and "looks like a Diablo clone" store rejections (Google Play and Apple both remove apps that impersonate or mislead, see [Play IP policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072?hl=en-GB)).
> Registration status of individual in-game terms was **not verified** (registers blocked, see TITLE_CHECK.md §1). Treat everything below as blocked regardless.

## 9. "Rift": specific assessment
- **Common noun.** "Rift" is a dictionary word and no one owns it for describing a crack or tear. In-world prose ("a rift opened in the swamp") is **low risk**.
- **Marks in our class.** **RIFT** is a registered mark of gamigo for its MMORPG ([gamigo legal notices](https://www.trionworlds.com/en/legal/legal-notices/)). Riot uses *Riftbound* and *Summoner's Rift*. Meta used *Oculus Rift* for VR hardware. As a **product title or title element**, "Rift" is **high risk**.
- **Diablo association.** As the **capitalized name of a timed-dungeon mode** ("Rifts", "Greater Rifts", "Rift Guardian", "Rift Keystone", "Challenge Rift"), it reproduces the Diablo III vocabulary bundle together with the mechanic. That is the passing-off and "clone" risk we want to avoid.
- **Verdict:** Do **not** use "Rift" as a mode, currency or UI label. Use our own name for timed dungeons (candidates: *Wells*, *Deep Wells*, *Dim Hollows*, *Lantern Runs*; each needs a legal check before adoption). "Rift" may appear in lore prose only.
